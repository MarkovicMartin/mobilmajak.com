MOBILMAJAK - Prerov - brana kamer (Windows PC v prodejne)
=========================================================

Pilot prodejna_id = 4. Stejny model jako Globus / Vsetin.

SIT:
  NVR:    10.0.0.90
  Kamera: 10.0.0.3  (brana komunikuje jen s NVR)

USB musi obsahovat OBE slozky:
  camera-gateway\
  prerov-gateway\   (s config.json)

--- PRIPRAVA NA MACU ---

  cp secrets/camera_motion_prerov.json scripts/prerov-gateway/config.json

  Na USB zkopirovat:
    scripts/camera-gateway/
    scripts/prerov-gateway/

--- INSTALACE (CMD jako SPRAVCE) ---

  cd C:\Temp\prerov-gateway
  install-prerov-camera-gateway.cmd

  Potreba INTERNET pri prvni instalaci (~25 MB Python).
  V NVR: Detekce -> Propojeni -> Upozornit iVMS u kanalu kamery.

  Volitelne (overeni IP): 02-discover-lan.cmd

--- OPRAVA POZDĚJI ---

  fix-and-restart-prerov.cmd

--- KONTROLA ---

  Log: C:\ProgramData\Mobilmajak\CameraGateway-Prerov\gateway.log
  Ulohy: Mobilmajak-CameraGateway-Prerov (+ WakeKick, akce wscript.exe)
  MOBILMAJAK: Smeny -> Neni v praci -> majacek u Prerova

  Vypnout spanek PC.

Pokud instalace selze: smazat C:\ProgramData\Mobilmajak\CameraGateway-Prerov a zkusit znovu.
