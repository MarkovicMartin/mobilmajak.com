MOBILMAJAK - Globus - brana kamer (Windows PC v prodejne)
==========================================================

Pilot prodejna_id = 1. Stejny model jako Sternberk / Zlin / Senimo.

SIT:
  NVR:    10.0.0.250
  Kamera: 192.168.254.3  (v NVR, brana komunikuje jen s NVR)

USB musi obsahovat OBE slozky:
  camera-gateway\
  globus-gateway\   (s config.json)

--- PRIPRAVA NA MACU (jednou) ---

  cp -R scripts/camera-gateway scripts/globus-gateway /Volumes/USB/
  cp secrets/camera_motion_globus.json /Volumes/USB/globus-gateway/config.json

--- NOVA INSTALACE (CMD jako SPRAVCE) ---

  cd C:\Temp\globus-gateway
  install-globus-camera-gateway.cmd

  Potreba INTERNET pri prvni instalaci (~25 MB Python).
  V NVR: Detekce -> Propojeni -> Upozornit iVMS u kanalu kamery.

--- OPRAVA / AKTUALIZACE (jiz nainstalovane PC) ---

  cd C:\Temp\globus-gateway
  fix-and-restart-globus.cmd

  Nebo jen skripty bez prepisu configu:
  update-installed-gateway.cmd

--- KONTROLA ---

  Log:
    C:\ProgramData\Mobilmajak\CameraGateway-Globus\gateway.log
  Ocekavano: Pripojeno HTTP 200, nasloucham, stale nasloucham ~60 s

  Ulohy Planovace:
    Mobilmajak-CameraGateway-Globus
    Mobilmajak-CameraGateway-Globus-WakeKick
    (akce = wscript.exe)

  MOBILMAJAK: Smeny -> Neni v praci -> majacek u Globusu (zdroj gateway).

Doporuceni: vypnout spanek PC.

Test z Macu (VPN do LAN):
  ./scripts/camera_motion_test_globus.sh isapi
  ./scripts/camera_motion_test_globus.sh motion true
